Overview

Streaming robot task understanding from synchronized views.

RoboChrono evaluates how vision-language and embodied AI systems interpret real robot behavior as it unfolds. Each episode pairs synchronized egocentric and external camera streams with temporally grounded annotations, enabling online recognition, progress estimation, spatial reasoning, and fine-grained step ordering under realistic robot execution dynamics.

Evaluation

Evaluation protocols for online understanding and model comparison.

01

Multi-view Encoding

Models receive aligned videos from external and wrist-mounted viewpoints, requiring robust fusion across complementary perspectives.

02

Online State Estimation

Predictions are evaluated as the stream progresses, testing whether models can maintain task state without access to future frames.

03

Step-level Metrics

The benchmark supports fine-grained assessment of recognition, temporal alignment, ordering, trajectory reasoning, and progress estimation.
